Who else from TIET is on the 6 pm bus?

Post your trip with roughly when you're leaving. If another TIET student is going to the same city within three hours of you, they show up on your trip with their first name, batch and departure time.

Send them a request. If they accept, you can chat here and agree where to meet. Until then, neither of you sees the other's full name or phone number.

If you're a woman, you can make a trip women-only. Then only women from TIET can see it or ask to join.
